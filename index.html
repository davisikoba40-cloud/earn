Short, plain-language summaries of how Mobilearn works.

Terms of service

Mobilearn is a platform operated by Mobilelabs UG for paid online tasks, YouTube video rewards and skill courses. Accounts are personal and may not be shared. Signup is limited to Airtel Uganda mobile numbers. One-time activation is UGX 19,500 via Airtel *185*9# using Merchant Code 7285061 (MOBILE LABS). YouTube video rewards are paid once per video per account. Misuse, fake submissions or duplicate accounts may result in suspension.

Privacy policy

We collect only what's needed to run your account: username, Airtel phone number and password. We never sell your data. Phone numbers are used for verification and support only.

Payout policy

Withdrawals are available from $5 to bank, mobile money, PayPal or crypto. Withdrawals are gated by Merchant Code 7285061 for security. Most payouts are processed within 24 hours. Earnings vary by task availability, quality score and region.
